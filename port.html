FAQ

Questions, Answered.

What video editing services do you offer?

Reels and Shorts, YouTube videos, cinematic edits, promotional videos, event highlights, color work, captions and motion graphics.

Do you work with beginners and small businesses?

Yes. I work with new creators, influencers, startups, small businesses and personal brands.

Are your prices affordable?

I aim to keep pricing friendly for different budgets. The final quote depends on the project scope.

Can I request a custom editing style?

Absolutely. Share reference videos and your preferred style, and we'll shape the edit around your vision.

Do you provide content creation and digital marketing?

Yes, including content ideas, planning, content calendars and social media creative support.

How do I share my footage and project files?

Send a cloud link (Google Drive, Dropbox, etc.) once we've agreed on the project. We'll confirm the best method together.

How many revisions are included?

Revisions are agreed upfront as part of the project scope, so expectations are clear for both sides.

How can I start a project?

Fill in the contact form or message me on WhatsApp or Instagram with your idea. I'll reply to discuss details, budget and timeline.
